Popup, window, or side panel

The same screen opens in three ways. It has the same accounts and the same codes in each one.

ViewHow it behavesGood for
PopupOpens under the toolbar icon. It closes when you click elsewhere.A quick copy of one code. This is the default.
Separate windowA small window that stays open, with a size and position it remembers.Keeping codes next to another app or the browser.
Side panelSits beside the page and stays open while you work.Keeping codes in view next to the site you sign in to.

Open each one

Choose what the toolbar icon opens

  1. Select "Settings" (the gear) in the popup header.
  2. Select "Display · Sync".
  3. Under "Open as", choose "Popup", "Separate window", or "Side panel".

When you choose "Popup", a "Popup size" row appears with "Small", "Medium", and "Large".

Open the window from the popup

  • Select the "Pop out" button in the header. The popup closes and the window opens.
  • Or right-click the toolbar icon and select "Open in a separate window".
  • Or assign a key to the "Open in a separate window" command in your browser's extension shortcut settings. It has no key by default.

The "Pop out" button is also in the side panel. It is hidden inside the window itself.

The separate window

  • It starts at 400 × 640. Resize or move it, and Popout opens it there next time.
  • Only one window exists. If it is already open, opening it again brings it to the front.
  • If the saved spot is off-screen (for example, you unplugged a monitor), it opens without a saved position.

List-only view

In the window, select "Show list only" (the small button at the top right) to hide the header, search, and tips. Only names and codes remain. Select "Show full view" to go back. You can also turn it on in "Display · Sync" with "Separate window · Show list only". It does not apply to the popup or the side panel.

1.0.1 From version 1.0.1, in Firefox, moving the separate window also remembers its position.

The side panel and browser support

Choose "Side panel" under "Open as", then click the toolbar icon to open it.

  • Chrome and Edge: the icon opens the browser's side panel.
  • Firefox: the icon opens a sidebar.
  • Safari on macOS: there is no side panel. Use the popup or the separate window.

If "Side panel" isn't listed under "Open as", your browser doesn't support it, and the popup is used instead.

Search and keyboard

  1. Open the list. The cursor is already in "Search".
  2. Type part of a service name, account name, group, or note. A "Results" header shows how many accounts match.
  3. Press ↑ or ↓ to select, Enter to copy the code, and Esc to clear the search.

Typing anywhere in the list moves you to "Search". Clicking a row also copies its code, and a "Copied" message appears.

  • Search in: next to the search box, "Search in" lets you look in "All", "Service", "Account", "Group", or "Note" only.
  • Several words: type more than one word, and an account must contain every word to match.
  • Secrets are never searched.

Groups

Groups keep related accounts together, such as "Work". A group name can be up to 64 characters. Each account has one group.

  1. On the account row, select ⋯ (or right-click) → "Edit".
  2. Type a name in "Group" and select "Save".

Once an account has a group, a row of chips appears: "All" and one chip per group. Select a chip to show only that group.

1.0.1 From version 1.0.1, the popup shows accounts under group headers instead of chips. Each header has the group name and a count. Select it to fold or unfold the group. Accounts without a group are under "No group". In the account menu, "Group" lets you pick a group, create one with "New group" (type a "Group name"), or choose "Remove from group". While you search, the list is flat.

On a group header, "Rename group" and "Ungroup" apply to all accounts in that group at once.

Order and important accounts

  • Reorder: drag a row up or down. With keyboard focus on a row, press Alt+↑ or Alt+↓. Your order is kept. Copying a code or visiting a site does not move rows.
  • Important: in the account's "Edit" screen, turn on "Mark as important". A ★ appears on the row ("Important account"). It does not change the order.
  • A–Z: below the search box, "A–Z" shows the list sorted by service and account name. Your own order is kept and comes back when you turn "A–Z" off. Drag reordering is paused while it is on.

Compact view and hidden codes

In "Display · Sync", "View" has three choices: "Normal", "Compact", and "Hide codes". "Compact" tightens the rows. "Hide codes" shows names only; select a row to copy its code. You can also press Alt+Shift+H in the list to hide or show codes. Other options on this screen are "Theme", "Zoom", "Language", and "Show colored initials before accounts".

On an account's "Edit" screen you can also choose one of 18 built-in line icons as buttons: Key, Mail, Work, Bank, Cloud, Code, Game, Shop, Social, Personal, Payment, Crypto, Server, Security, School, Home, Website, and Health. A chosen icon always shows in the popup, even when initial tiles are off. Icons are kept in Popout backups and sync.

The account menu

Select ⋯ on a row, right-click it, or press Shift+F10. The menu offers:

  • "Fill on this page" — see Fill codes into sign-in pages.
  • "Copy next code" — for accounts with a timer.
  • "Show QR" — to add the account on another device. With a password set, you enter it again first.
  • "Share codes" — see Share codes.
  • "Copy account name" — the name is not cleared from the clipboard automatically, because it is not a code.
  • "Edit" and "Account history".
  • "Delete" — moves the account to the trash after you confirm.

Account for the current site

When the page you are on matches an account, the row gets a "Fill" button. A match comes from the site addresses saved on the account or from where you used it before.

  • In the popup, Popout looks at the tab you opened it from.
  • In the window and the side panel, it asks "Let suggestions follow the tab you switch to?" Select "Allow" to follow your tabs, or "Dismiss".
  • After you copy a code on a site that isn't saved for that account, you may see "Remember this account for example.com?". Select "Remember" or "No".

To stop suggestions, turn off "Suggest accounts for sites" under "Copying and privacy" in Settings. Turning it off also clears the site usage history.

Trash

"Delete" asks: "Move this account to trash? It will be permanently deleted after 30 days." Select "Move to trash".

To remove several at once, select "Select" below the search box, tick the accounts (or use "Select all shown" to pick every account in the current list), and move them to the trash together after you confirm. The trash keeps them for 30 days.

  1. Select "Settings" → "Trash".
  2. Each account shows how many days are left, for example "29 days left". Select "Restore" to bring it back, or "Delete permanently".
  3. "Empty trash" removes everything in it for good.

For exporting accounts and restoring earlier versions, see Back up and sync.
